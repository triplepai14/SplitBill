Privacy Policy — TallyMore

Effective date: 18 July 2026 · App: TallyMore (com.magsenium.tallymore)

The short version

TallyMore does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data storage

All bills, people, categories and amounts you enter are saved in a local database inside the app's private storage on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server. The app works entirely offline and requires no account or sign-in.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting services, and makes no network requests.

Sharing feature

When you tap Share, the app hands a plain-text summary to the sharing feature of your operating system. You choose which app receives it (e.g. LINE). TallyMore itself never sends this data anywhere.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions — no location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data it stored, since the data exists only on your device.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes (for example, adding cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
